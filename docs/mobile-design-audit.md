# Mobile design audit: the desktop and web feel on the phone (v2)

Date: 2026-10-30. Branch: `design/mobile-v2`, cut from `main` (1.6.0, `553cc0e`) with `design/apple-redesign-mobile` (v1) merged in; local, not merged.
Tested on: the iOS 26 Simulator (iPhone 17) running the EAS development build, and the Pixel 8 emulator (Android 17) running a local debug build, both against production in read-only dev mode (section 5). Screenshots are kept outside the repository (they show real club, player and customer names). The approved mockups are a Design canvas (fake names) linked from `design-audit/mobile-v2/decisions.md`, also outside the repository.

## 1. Summary

**Before (`main`):** hand-built chrome, Barlow Condensed titles, twelve colour tokens, Unicode glyphs as icons, a portrait-only player, RN `<Modal>` sheets, never built for Android.

**v1 (2026-10-09/10, `design/apple-redesign-mobile`):** the web's phone layout inside native chrome per platform (NativeTabs, large titles, an iOS header search bar, Material on Android), SF Symbols and Material Symbols, the web's derived colours and text styles. The native shell worked; the screens did not. Next to desktop and web the founder found it unfinished and cluttered: everything a bordered card, feed rows with up to eight controls, filters stacked above the content, secondary text nearly the size of body text, primary tints at five strengths, nine Tailwind colour families on the clip list.

**v2 (this branch):** the desktop's calm, the way the Claude and Notion apps read: the system font on the desktop/web scale, one accent used sparingly, content as plain rows on the page, settings as grouped lists, filters and sort behind one button, and the app's own chrome, the same on iOS and Android.

## 2. Decisions

The founder's (2026-10-29/30):

- **Type:** Inter (2026-10-30, chosen over the system font after a side-by-side on the mockup canvas: the Notion feel), on the web's touch scale with Inter's own size-specific tracking. The large title, title 2 and the headline are semibold (600). Inter is free (SIL Open Font License), bundled from `@expo-google-fonts/inter` and loaded by the root layout; each weight is its own font file, so `Text` picks the file from the weight (`src/lib/fonts.ts`). Claude's own faces (Anthropic Sans and Serif) can't be licensed.
- **Navigation and lists as the Claude app** (2026-10-30, round 3, from the founder's screenshots of Claude's Cowork, Artifacts, Chats and sidebar): a **sidebar** replaces the tab bar (expo-router's Drawer, slide type, JS only): the wordmark, the club switcher, the destinations (players: My playlists, My highlights; coaches: Shared by me and Shared with me, two views of `/playlists` through a `view` param), Recent playlists, and the account row that opens Profile. Root screens have a centred small title, the menu button and the filter button in glass circles; no large titles. Filters and sort open a **pop-up menu** from the filter button (`PopoverMenu`: checks, icons, counts, groups split by hairlines). **Search** is a floating capsule at the foot of the list.
- **Shared with me as Cowork** (the founder: players should see this week's scouting first): sections by when it was shared (This week, Last week, then months; `shareTimeSections`, tested), rows on the page with a status glyph (empty ring new, part ring started, check watched), the team above the name when the feed mixes sources, "New · coach · clips · when" or "coach · 2 of 6 watched", and a primary dot while new. Tapping a started playlist resumes it; the next one to watch is a floating pill ("Resume" / "Watch new"). **Shared by me as Artifacts**: one card per playlist with a tile ("1/7 finished" over the web's watch bar), the name, where it went and when, grouped by week and month while sorted by Recently shared; tapping opens the recipients in a sheet (Remind, Remind all, Watch as a player). Pattern sources: Claude's apps, Google Classroom's turned-in counts, Material's three-line item, NN/g on cards, inbox unread cues, podcast apps' progress on started items.
- **Settings as a sheet** (2026-10-30, round 4, from the founder's screenshots of Claude's settings): the account row opens Settings as a sheet over the app, the native page sheet on iOS and a full-screen page sliding up on Android (Material's full-screen dialog), with its own stack inside. The first page is short groups whose rows show their value and open a page (Team, Devices, Theme, Send feedback), the account card on top that opens Profile (as iOS Settings), Light / Dark / System as preview tiles in the user's own themes, and Sign out apart, in red, asking first. Profile edits the name in place (saved with the check in the bar; leaving unsaved asks first); the photo waits for a store build (an image picker is a native module). Sources: Apple's HIG on sheets (close leading, Done trailing, Back never dismisses, one sheet at a time, confirm unsaved changes on swipe), Android's settings pattern (an overview of the most used settings, subscreens titled as their rows, status instead of descriptions, no version on the overview), and the usual rule that destructive actions go last and apart but never hidden.
- **Where a playlist comes from:** once a player's feed mixes teams (or a team and personal shares), each row names its team, or "Just for you", in a footnote line above the name (`feedSourceLabel`, `feedHasSeveralSources`, tested).
- **Chrome:** the app's own, identical on both platforms (since round 3: the sidebar, glass buttons and centred small titles above). No NativeTabs, no native large titles, no header search bar. On Android with gesture navigation the screen's left edge belongs to the system's back gesture, so the sidebar opens from its button there.
- **Density:** content first. Filters and sort behind one title-bar button that opens a pop-up menu; no roll-up sentence on Shared by me (its numbers are the menu's counts); no filled "New" pills.
- **Android bars** are solid (Material's idiom; expo-blur there re-renders a blur target every scroll frame). iOS blurs, except under Reduce Transparency.

Made while building (each in the plan's ledger with its cost): icons are lucide (`lucide-react-native` at the web's version, JS only), the same glyphs and 1.75 stroke as desktop and web; menus are the app's own pop-up menu and bigger choices (the theme picker, a playlist's recipients) the app's own `Sheet`, rather than platform menus or native sheets (Feedback and Delete account were native form sheets until round 4 made them pages inside the settings sheet: no sheet over a sheet); settings screens sit on a grouped floor (`canvas`, the `muted` tone where a light theme's cards equal its page) while content screens stay on the page colour; hairlines are a device pixel.

## 4. Behaviour changes

All presentation except the name editing of round 4; every `trackEvent` name and count matches `main` (checked at each milestone). Round 4 added routes inside the settings sheet (`/profile/details`, `/profile/team`, `/profile/devices`, `/profile/theme`, `/profile/feedback`, `/profile/delete-account`), so `page_viewed` reports those paths; `/feedback` and `/delete-account` were sheets left out of page views, and now count as pages under `/profile/`.

- Filters and sort moved from segmented controls and pop-ups into one pop-up menu per list (each group's first row resets it); the filter button turns primary while any is on, and a footnote line names them with Clear.
- The feed groups by week and month instead of New / In progress / Watched (now filters). Up next became a floating pill; a started row resumes where it stopped.
- Search is a floating capsule at the foot of the list on both platforms, shown from eight playlists (the coach's dashboard always).
- Shared by me: the three stat cells became the filter menu's counts; cards open a recipients sheet with per-player and per-playlist reminders and "Watch as a player"; bulk reminder results are toasts (failures still alert).
- The player's upright layout lost its three big centre buttons and corner buttons for the same glass bar the wide layout had; the clip list lost its progress bar.
- Profile: inline Change…/Delete… buttons became rows; the theme swatches moved into a sheet; Sign out and Delete account form the last group.
- Settings (round 4): Profile is no longer a sidebar destination but a sheet over the app (the sidebar's account row, `scoutable://profile`); X or a swipe down closes it. The name can be edited on mobile. Sign out asks first. Light / Dark / System are tiles instead of a segmented control. Team, Devices and the theme swatches are pages instead of groups and a sheet. Feedback and Delete account are pages inside the sheet without a Cancel button (Back does it). A cold `scoutable://profile` (app not running) opens Settings full screen, and X goes to the playlists.
- The tab bar is gone: the sidebar holds the destinations, the coach's two views (no segmented control), Recent playlists, the club switcher (moved from Profile) and Profile (the account row). The red count of new playlists became a dot on the menu button and a count in the sidebar; the app-icon badge is unchanged.

## 5. Verification

- **Each milestone:** `npm run typecheck` (mobile, shared; web and desktop when shared changed), the shared tests (1352), the analytics comparison with `main`, the outbound-link grep, and runtime checks on both devices.
- **Read-only mode:** Metro ran with `EXPO_PUBLIC_READ_ONLY=1` and empty PostHog and Sentry keys; every write was refused and logged (watches, reminders, device touches, theme sync).
- **Runtime, both platforms:** the tab bar and its badge; the title bar's hand-off and material while scrolling, and after switching tabs; menu and filter sheets opened, applied, reset, dragged closed (iOS) and closed with Android back; the feed's rows and filters; the dashboard's rows, opened recipients and filter sheet; the watch view, playback with the glass bar and timeline, full screen in landscape and Done back to upright; Profile and the theme sheet (Courtside applied and set back); the team picker; an invalid invite's status screen; the feedback sheet over the keyboard; large text (iOS accessibility-medium, Android font scale 1.3).
- **Grep gates:** no `expo-symbols`, `@expo/ui`, `expo-glass-effect` or `@expo/vector-icons` import; no radius outside the policy (the player bar's 12 pt is the one exception); no retired component.

Not verified: the signed-out screens, onboarding, no-org, the device gate and the error screen at runtime (signing out would end the test sessions; typecheck and reading only); Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency at runtime (springs use `ReduceMotion.System`; `Material` and `PlayerMaterial` go solid); VoiceOver and TalkBack passes; a physical iPhone or Android phone; iPad; push end to end; a real join, reminder, feedback report or account deletion; Sign in with Apple. Round 3 was checked on Android only (the iOS simulator was in use): the sidebar's edge swipe, the blurred glass buttons, menu and capsule, and the capsule rising with an on-screen keyboard (the emulator shows none) are unverified on iOS. Round 4 likewise (Android, light, dark, font scale 1.4, TalkBack roles from the accessibility tree): the iOS page sheet itself, its insets and the swipe-down hold while a name is unsaved wait for an iOS pass; a successful name save was not run (read-only mode refuses it; the failure path, the discard prompt and the unit tests were).

## 6. Known gaps and next steps

- **Store build.** `expo-symbols`, `@expo/ui`, `expo-glass-effect` and `@expo/vector-icons` are still in `package.json` so the installed dev clients keep matching (the native fingerprint is unchanged on this branch). Remove them (keep `expo-font`: it loads Inter), set the `expo-notifications` colour to the primary, align lucide across the three apps, then build. Publish `main`'s pending OTA before merging: after the merge the runtime fingerprint changes.
- **iOS title bar edge:** RN has no mask-image, so the blur ends hard and a 10 pt gradient of the page colour stands in for the web's fade.
- **iOS refresh spinner** shows through the clear bar while pulling and sits partly under the status bar text.
- **Text size changed while the app is open** can leave text clipped until relaunch (RN keeps the old measurements).
- **Maestro flows** live in `design-audit/mobile/flows` (outside the repository); `m6-profile.yaml` walks the settings sheet (Profile, Team, Devices, Theme, Send feedback, Delete account, each closed with Back). Maestro takes whole-number percentages only.

### What a Google Play release would take

The app builds and runs on Android 17, and edge-to-edge, the keyboard, back handling, sheets, menus and full screen were checked on the emulator. To ship it:

1. **A Play Console app** for `se.scoutable.app`, with an upload key managed by EAS (`eas credentials -p android`) and an `android` section in `eas.json`'s production profile (app bundle).
2. **Push:** `google-services.json` is already in the app; the FCM server key or v1 credentials go into EAS for `expo-notifications`.
3. **App links:** `assetlinks.json` on app.scoutable.se for invite and playlist links (iOS uses universal links for the same).
4. **Sign in with Apple** runs through the browser on Android (the Apple service ID's return URL must allow the app's redirect); Google sign-in works as on iOS.
5. **Store listing:** screenshots, the Data safety form (account data, watch history, crash reports, analytics), a content rating, the privacy policy URL, target API level as Play requires.
6. **Policy:** keep the same club-only, no-purchase, no-outbound-link stance as on iOS; Play's payments policy treats links to web purchases much like App Review does.
7. **Testing on real devices** through an internal testing track before production.
